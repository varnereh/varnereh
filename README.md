## 🙋🏼‍♂️ About Me
Born in Ohio, I've lived in Versailles, Ohio for the majority of my life. Ever since I could remember, I have always been fascinated with technology and coding. Things like video games, websites, and phones always struck my interest, especially with my unyielding curiosity of how they worked. As I got older, I took AP Computer Science Principles in high school, and this absolutely skyrocketed my interest. Here, I got to work on my first programming languages: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Since then, I have been able to explore my capabilities with coding, making personal and school-related projects. With my love for music, games, and coding, I do hope to one day employ my skills and create a large project like a video game. Regardless, I have some neat projects linked at the top of this page.
